ABOUT BRAND

Personal Explorations is a self-initiated graphic design and poster series exploring dynamic typography, Swiss-punk layout grids, and high-contrast comic halftone treatments inspired by Riot Games' VALORANT, Japanese indie music, and contemporary anime culture. Spanning iconic tactical agent spotlights (Cypher, Viper, Jett, Yoru) and immersive UI concepts to the atmospheric 'Radio Eva' visual and expressive typographic artworks, each piece experiments with brutalist type compositions, textured distress overlays, bilingual accents, and vibrant color interplay to push visual storytelling beyond conventional boundaries.
