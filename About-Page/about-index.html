ABOUT

I am a multi-disciplinary designer and artist based in Indonesia. For over seven years, from my early days in vocational school to my formal graphic design studies in college. I’ve immersed myself in this craft.

To me, design has always been a tool for everyday life. If there is one core truth I’ve learned over the years, it’s that design is fundamentally about communication. It is the visual language we use to translate complex problems into clear solutions, and honestly, finding that translation is what I enjoy the most. I bring that exact mindset to every client I work with, using the skills I’ve honed over the years to craft unique, meaningful designs that don't just look good, but actually work.
